# Deep find A2: fixes from the UK Residential Management test — Design

Date: 2026-10-20. Author: Claude (architect). Trigger: Kevin's live test on "UK Residential Management Limited" (UKRM) after Build A. Evidence from the database: the run marked the lead's own site `ukrmanagement.com` as "a different business" (title is "UKRM Home", an abbreviation of the legal name; the footer/title share no word with the registered name), so the domain was never confirmed and Deep find skipped email guessing for both directors ("No company domain is confirmed"); the lead's own phone, address, postcode and `info@ukrmanagement.com` were all on that site; the latest run spent 13 Hunter credits (12 on "company domains") on a lead that already had a website; the same director appears twice ("Karthik Venkatapuram" from Companies House and "Karthik Reddy Venkatapuram" from the web); in the first run the exact-name active company (09238000, "exact name, active, registered location matches") was shown as "Most likely" but not auto-picked. Parent: `2026-10-19-deepfind-effort-and-matching-design.md`.

## Rules (binding, unchanged)
Review-before-send; auto-apply blank-only with Undo; wrong auto-picks (company or domain) are the costliest failure, so when unsure ask; worker/Hunter/AI/Firecrawl/Apollo/Places guards and per-org caps; secrets never logged; plain English; accessibility; no migration in this build (if one is needed, stop and record the question).

## Part 1: website suspicion false positives (the root cause for UKRM)
`websiteSuspicion` (`supabase/functions/_shared/domainDiscovery.ts`) flags "title and footer name a different business" when no distinctive word of the lead/registered name appears. Add corroboration so a correct site is not rejected:
- Abbreviations: an acronym or abbreviation of the registered/lead name (initials of its words, e.g. UK Residential Management -> UKRM; initials with a trailing word, e.g. "UKR Mgmt"; and the domain label (`ukrmanagement`) being a concatenation/abbreviation of name tokens (UK + R(esidential) + management)) counts as a name match in the title/footer/domain.
- Contact-detail corroboration (strong): if the site text contains the lead's saved phone number (digits, ignoring spaces/+44/0) OR the lead's postcode, OR the lead's saved email address/domain, the site is NOT suspect on name grounds alone (record the evidence in `website_reasons` as "confirmed by phone/postcode/email on the site"). A foreign-phone-number or other independent suspicion reasons still apply.
- When the site is no longer suspect, the domain follows the existing confirmation rules ("the lead's website, a contact's email") so the common-format guesses and the checker run for the people found.
Tests with the UKRM case (title "UKRM Home", domain ukrmanagement.com, lead phone 020 8577 0450, postcode TW3 3TT), a genuinely different business (must stay suspect), a directory page (must stay suspect), and the existing suspicion tests unchanged.

## Part 2: company auto-pick regression test (reproduce, then fix whatever blocks)
Add a test with the exact data: lead name "UK Residential Management Limited", city London, postcode TW3 3TT (also "TW33TT"), address "Unit 1, Bellview Court, 179-183 Hanworth Rd, Hounslow"; candidates: UK RESIDENTIAL MANAGEMENT LTD 09238000 (active, TW3 3TT), AFCG RESIDENTIAL MANAGEMENT LIMITED, ALBION RIVERSIDE RESIDENTIAL MANAGEMENT LIMITED, ABBEY LANE RESIDENTIAL MANAGEMENT LIMITED (all active, London/other addresses, location agreeing only by city "London" or not at all), ABBOTTSFORD RESIDENTIAL MANAGEMENT COMPANY LIMITED. Expected: auto-pick 09238000 (sole active exact-name match with E2 postcode/address agreement; the others do not carry the word "UK" and must not block). Find why it is not picked (possible causes: weak city-only location agreement counting for other candidates in tie logic, `websiteSuspicion` blocking, the evidence `strength` threshold) and fix without weakening the safeguards (never pick inactive, ties ask, different-name candidate with E3/E4 blocks). Also make E2 distinguish postcode/address agreement (strong) from city-only agreement (weak): city-only must not count as E2 for tie or block purposes on its own.

## Part 3: waste and duplicates
- Hunter company-domain searches: cap at 2 per lead when the lead already has a website or a saved email domain that is confirmed, and at the effort-level share otherwise (Medium 1, High 3, Extra high 6 as in the effort table; Custom: min(existing 12, run cap share)); stop searching as soon as a domain is confirmed. Test that a lead with a confirmed own site spends at most 2.
- Same-person de-duplication for DISPLAY and contact creation (`findSamePerson` and the suggestion/officer lists): treat people as the same when the first name and the last name match ignoring middle names and title (Karthik Venkatapuram == Karthik Reddy Venkatapuram), including across sources (Companies House, web, site, manual); keep the fullest name; never merge on last name alone or different first names; auto owner/title WRITES still require an exact full-name match (unchanged safety rule).

## Part 4: email finding when the checker cannot confirm
- Provider detection: classify the domain's mail provider from MX records already looked up (`dnsLookup.ts`): Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, other known hosted providers, unknown. Say it plainly in the person's line: "uk-residential... uses Microsoft 365, which does not allow mailboxes to be checked, so these are best guesses." (fixed sentences, no provider text).
- Ranking: for each known person on a confirmed domain whose guesses cannot be verified (catch-all, Google/Microsoft, checker unavailable), show ONE clearly labelled "Most likely address" (first of the common formats, boosted by the format seen on any public address of that domain) with a one-click Add (a suggestion, unverified label, never auto-applied) and the other candidates collapsed under "Other possible formats". Use the order first.last, first, flast, firstlast, first_last unless a known address on the domain shows another format.
- Public format discovery (free steps first): from addresses already seen on the domain (lead email, scraped addresses, contacts) infer the format (existing `inferDomainPattern`); NEW: at High / Extra high effort, one grounded web search for addresses on the confirmed domain ("@domain") to learn the format, counted in the AI budget; never fetch private data.
- Hunter domain search remains the paid way to get real addresses; do not spend it when the free steps already have a verified address.
Tests for provider classification, ranking, the Medium path with a Microsoft 365 domain (no verified address, one "Most likely" suggestion), and that nothing is ever auto-applied from an unverifiable guess.
