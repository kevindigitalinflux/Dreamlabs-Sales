## Goal and the flow (Kevin, 2026-11-11)
A rep does TWO rounds a day: (1) multi-select up to 50 leads, run Deep find; (2) multi-select the leads that came out ready, multi-draft, review the drafts in bulk and release. About 100 outreach emails per day. **No new "hands-off" button and no new run type.** The existing Deep find, multi-draft and Waiting to release screens must become strong and tight enough that this flow needs almost no per-lead review.

## Evidence (Claude, production, read-only, 2026-11-11)
345 leads; only 51 ever assessed (5 ready, 14 almost, 32 needs work); 294 never had a Deep find. Among the 51: `verified_email` missing 40, `confirmed_website` 37, `company_match` 7, `decision_maker` 4. 251 UK leads have no company number (8 rejected). SMTP cannot confirm Microsoft 365 / Google-hosted mailboxes, so a guessed `first.last@domain` stays "unverified" forever and readiness punishes it. The 2026-11-05 send bounced about 10% (7 bad of 72 recipients), so sender reputation needs a guard.

## Kevin's decisions (2026-11-11; authorisation for the described behaviour only)
1. **Auto-send rule: strong evidence plus well-supported guesses** (tiers T1 and T2 below) may be saved and drafted without per-lead review, inside the existing send window, daily caps, Pause/Stop, pre-send checker and the reputation guard. T3 goes to a short "needs a look" filter.
2. **Company match: auto-pick on AI web proof** at every effort level (Low included); ask the user only when the evidence conflicts.
3. **No new button.** Improve Deep find, readiness, multi-draft and release.

## Evidence tiers for a recipient address (one pure module, tested, used everywhere)
- **T1 strong:** checker `valid`; replied before (`email_outcomes.replied`); published by the company itself (its own site, Places listing, or the lead's own saved non-guessed email); or **sent_ok**: the address received an email from us and no hard bounce arrived within 72 hours (item 4).
- **T2 supported guess:** a generated address whose format is shared by at least two T1 addresses of DIFFERENT people at the same domain (the existing "Pattern-supported" rule), domain has a mail server, not a role address on a hosted/catch-all domain.
- **T3 weak:** any other guess, role address on hosted/catch-all, unverifiable with no supporting pattern.
- **Never sendable:** `bounced_hard`, checker `invalid`, no mail server, bad syntax, opted out, blocklisted.

3. **Auto-apply on Deep find.** When Deep find finds a T1 or T2 address for a person or the company, it saves it as the lead's usable recipient (lead email when blank, otherwise the contact `is_primary`), so multi-draft works with no per-lead review. T3 stays a suggestion. Respect existing safeguards: never overwrite typed values, blank-only fills, `include_in_sequences` rules (found contacts default OFF today: report what must change for the FIRST email to go to a T1/T2 contact while follow-up enrolment stays per existing rules and ask-before-enrolling policy), dismissed suggestions stay dismissed, hard-bounced/blocked never applied. 5. **Bounce-driven fallback format.** When a person's guessed address hard-bounces, queue (as a normal Waiting-to-release draft, never auto-sent) a draft to the next-ranked format for that person; at most 2 attempts per person ever; skip formats already bounced; respect the reputation guard (item 7).
